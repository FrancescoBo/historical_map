Historical borders

    Click an area on the map to color the whole state it belongs to, colonies included, with a different color for each state. Click a colored area again to remove the whole state. Change a color with its swatch. "Color all states" gives neighbouring states (even across a narrow strait) different colors and keeps the ones you chose. Territory names are the official English names of the time, with the controlling state in brackets; to add a state you can also type its common or earlier name (Turkey, Siam…). A lighter color marks a disputed territory, shown in the color of whoever controls it de facto. Clicking a territory also shows a link to its Wikipedia page.

    Clips the historical map to the real coastline and shows islands even where the dataset has none. Untick to see the original borders.

    Requires the detailed coastline. Relief: Esri (hillshade); rivers and lakes: Natural Earth. Rivers switch from 1:50m to the more detailed 1:10m as you zoom in.

    Without the tick, the file is an area drawn in the chosen color over the base map.
